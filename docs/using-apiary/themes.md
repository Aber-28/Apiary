# Themes

Apiary comes with eleven looks. Change it from the **palette button** at the top right, or on **Settings, Appearance**. The choice is remembered in that browser.

| Theme | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Dark** and **Bright** | The defaults. Apiary starts in the one your device prefers. |
| **Matrix** | Green on black with a monospace font and softly falling code behind the page. The rain stops by itself if your device asks for reduced motion, and while the tab is hidden. |
| **Glass** and **Glass Dark** | Frosted, see-through panels over a soft colourful wallpaper, with rounded corners. On a browser without blur support the panels turn solid. |
| **Nord**, **Tokyo Night**, **Dracula** | Popular dark palettes. |
| **Solarized Light**, **Graphite** | Calm, businesslike light palettes. |
| **High contrast** | Black and white with a bright yellow accent and thick outlines, for maximum legibility. |

Every theme was checked for readable text: body text, muted text, links, buttons and status colours all meet the 4.5 to 1 contrast level.
