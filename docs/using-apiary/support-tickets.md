# Support tickets

Something wrong? Open **Support tickets**, write a short title and what happened, choose how urgent it is, and send it.

- By default your CEO handles it and passes it to the right person. You can choose someone yourself.
- The agent works on it and answers in plain English. If it needs something from you, the ticket stays *Open* and shows what it asks.
- When it is fixed the ticket says *Fixed. Please check*. If it is still broken, reply and the agent tries again. Otherwise close the ticket.
- Every ticket also appears on the Tasks board.
